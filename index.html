Human Capability Morphology

IWAS Avatar Examples

Different capability lives create different organisms.

These examples show how the IWAS Avatar can represent capability structure, evidence strength, validation and aspiration across different human lives.

The problem

The world still reduces people to CVs, degrees and job titles.

But human capability is richer than that. It grows through work, pressure, curiosity, relationships, practice, responsibility and lived experience. IWAS is being built to make that hidden capability visible, developable and connectable.

The individual journey

Aron has experience, but no formal path.

Aron has worked in restaurants, warehouses, retail and construction. He has learned to adapt, solve problems, handle pressure, work with difficult customers and keep teams moving. But traditional systems mostly see what he lacks: a degree, a title, a clean career ladder.

→

Through IWAS, Aron gradually builds his capability avatar from an underdeveloped blob into a richer organism: not by pretending to be someone else, but by revealing evidence of who he is becoming.

The opportunity journey

Elín is building a company and needs the right person.

Elín is an entrepreneur with a growing business. She needs someone who can learn fast, talk to customers, organize messy work, solve real problems and grow with the company. Traditional hiring pushes her toward narrow filters: degrees, old job titles and familiar CV patterns.

Learning agility Customer judgement Reliability Problem solving Growth potential

IWAS lets Elín define the capability shape of the opportunity itself — the real work, the real pressures and the human qualities needed to succeed.

Instead of asking only “Who has done this before?”, IWAS asks: “Whose capability organism fits this challenge — and who has the potential to grow into it?”

Scientific foundation

Built on evidence, not personality-test theatre.

Psychometric logic

Developmental evidence

Capability is treated as dynamic: shaped by experience, learning, context and growth trajectories.

Matching intelligence
